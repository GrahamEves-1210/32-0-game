About 32-0

32-0 is a free men's college basketball strategy game. Draft five players across eras and conferences, simulate a full season and NCAA Tournament, and see if your team can go 32-0.

How It Works

Players spin to receive a random conference and era combination, then draft one player per position — PG, SG, SF, PF, and C — from a pool of hundreds of real college basketball legends.

Features

Daily challenges, achievement badges, head-to-head challenges, a global leaderboard, and player stats spanning nine eras of college basketball history.
